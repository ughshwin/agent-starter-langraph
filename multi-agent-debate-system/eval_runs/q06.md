Ultimately, keeping jobs within Redis consolidates our architecture into one coherent data plane, streamlining troubleshooting, reducing incident surface area, and preserving engineering focus for business features rather than infrastructure churn.

_Key points:_
- Cost avoidance by eliminating a new broker
- Operational simplicity: one system to monitor, patch, and scale

**PROSECUTION — opening statement**

While it introduces additional operational overhead, the benefits in terms of durability, isolation from caching workloads, and ensuring exactly-once processing far outweigh these costs. _Key points:_

- Isolation: Separating job queues from Redis prevents resource contention and potential performance degradation in the caching layer.
