### Opening statements

**DEFENCE — opening statement**

By adopting a pooled shared‑schema architecture with robust row‑level security (RLS), the company can deliver the speed‑to‑market and cost advantages that mid‑market customers demand while still satisfying regulatory isolation for the regulated enterprise subset. RLS allows each tenant’s data to be logically isolated within a single database instance, eliminating the need for per‑tenant schemas or databases, thereby reducing operational overhead such as provisioning, patching, backup scheduling, and infrastructure scaling by orders of magnitude. The shared‑schema model also streamlines migration workflows; upgrades can be rolled out once across all tenants rather than individually, minimizing downtime risk and easing governance compliance. Finally, the cost savings from a single database cluster—lower license or cloud usage, fewer DBAs, reduced storage duplication—translate into a more competitive pricing structure that is essential for securing larger deals without sacrificing security or auditability.

_Key points:_
- Cost efficiency through a single database cluster
- Operational simplicity: provisioning, patching, and backup streamlined
- Scalable row‑level isolation satisfies compliance for regulated tenants
- Reduced migration risk and downtime with global upgrades

The Prosecution will argue that deploying a database-per-tenant silo model is the necessary choice for our B2B SaaS company, particularly given the stringent security and compliance requirements of our regulated enterprise clients. While the pooled shared-schema model may offer cost efficiencies and simplicity, it poses significant risks due to potential data exposure in case of a breach. Furthermore, the siloed approach provides unparalleled customization capabilities, allowing us to meet the unique needs of each tenant without compromising on security. This choice not only mitigates long-term operational and legal risks but also positions our company as a leader in compliance and data protection, essential for attracting and retaining enterprise clients.
