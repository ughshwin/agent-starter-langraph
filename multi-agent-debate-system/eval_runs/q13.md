### Opening statements

**DEFENCE — opening statement**

Eliminating per‑seat costs removes a hidden recurring expense that grows linearly with user base and staff expansion; the initial engineering effort is recouped quickly as the system matures.  Tailoring the solution to proprietary event streams and performance characteristics ensures no compromises on latency or data fidelity, something that generic vendor tooling cannot guarantee without costly workarounds.  Finally, self‑hosting places control of security, compliance, and incident response firmly in-house, preventing third‑party bottlenecks and exposing the platform only to its own trust boundaries.

_Key points:_
- Full architectural ownership and ability to evolve
- Cost savings from eliminating per‑seat licensing fees
- Custom data handling that aligns with internal infrastructure
- No vendor lock‑in or external request path, enhancing security
- Scalable maintenance model that grows with the organization


**PROSECUTION — opening statement**

Adopting a vendor like LaunchDarkly is the most strategic decision for this platform as it provides an immediate, mature solution that minimizes development and maintenance overhead while maximizing time-to-value. By leveraging a third-party provider, the team avoids the substantial upfront effort required to build and maintain an in-house feature-flagging system, which would divert critical resources away from core product development. The recurring costs associated with LaunchDarkly are predictable and scalable, ensuring that the platform can grow without the risk of unforeseen technical debt or operational burden. Furthermore, LaunchDarkly's expertise in managing experimentation and feature releases reduces exposure to security vulnerabilities and ensures compliance with industry standards, ultimately delivering a more reliable and secure solution for users.

_Key points:_
- Leverages mature, ready-to-use solution reducing development effort
- Predictable recurring costs with scalability benefits
