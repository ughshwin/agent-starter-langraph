### Opening statements

**DEFENCE — opening statement**

The team should self‑host using Qdrant to maintain full control, avoid per‑query costs, and ensure data remains in‑house despite the operational burden.  By running Qdrant on our existing infrastructure we eliminate recurring vendor fees that accrue with each query, preserving budget for future feature development instead of paying a subscription that will rise as usage grows.  Full ownership of the vector store guarantees that sensitive embeddings never leave the corporate perimeter, mitigating compliance risks and data‑privacy concerns that managed services cannot guarantee; this is essential when handling proprietary or regulated content.  Furthermore, Qdrant’s open‑source architecture affords us granular tuning—index configurations, shard placement, and hardware optimization—that would be invisible in a black‑box managed solution, enabling us to scale precisely to our performance requirements while keeping operational costs predictable.

_Key points:_
- Zero per‑query cost eliminates hidden spending as traffic increases
- Data sovereignty and compliance preserved by in‑house hosting
- Fine‑grained control over indexing and scaling for optimal performance
- Long‑term cost savings outweigh initial operational overhead


**PROSECUTION — opening statement**

The prosecution strongly advocates for adopting Pinecone as a managed service to accelerate deployment and offload scaling responsibilities. By leveraging Pinecone, the team can focus on their core business logic without the operational burden of managing infrastructure, ensuring faster time-to-market. The fully managed nature of Pinecone eliminates the need for complex setup and scaling decisions, which are critical for efficiency. While it's true that self-hosting options like PGVector or Qdrant offer control and avoid recurring costs, the trade-offs in terms of deployment speed and scalability make Pinecone the superior choice for this scenario.

_Key points:_
- Pinecone accelerates deployment by eliminating infrastructure setup complexity.
- Managed service offloads scaling responsibilities, ensuring seamless growth.
- Control over data is less critical than the efficiency gains from Pinecone's services.
- Recurring costs and third-party dependency are justified by the benefits of speed and scalability.
